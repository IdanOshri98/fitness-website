Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Koach ("we", "the app") is a fitness, nutrition and progress-tracking app. This page explains, in plain language, what information we collect, why, where it lives, and what control you have over it.

1. What we collect

When you create an account, we collect the email address and password you provide (handled entirely by our authentication provider, Supabase — we never see or store your password ourselves). As you use the app, we store the information you choose to enter, and nothing else:

  • Your workout program, exercises and logged sets (weight, reps, notes)
  • Your nutrition plan, meals and foods
  • Body profile details you provide (height, age, sex, activity level, goal) — only if and when you enter them
  • Weight and sleep entries you log

We never invent, guess, or pre-fill this information on your behalf — if a value is missing, the app asks you for it rather than assuming a number.

2. Where it's stored

Your data is stored in a database hosted by Supabase (built on PostgreSQL). Each user's data is protected by database-level access rules (Row Level Security) that only allow your own signed-in account to read or write it — not other users, and not the app's own public code. The app itself is hosted on Vercel. A copy of your data is also cached in your browser's local storage for offline resilience and speed.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. It is only processed by the infrastructure providers above (Supabase, Vercel) as necessary to run the app — they act as data processors, not independent users of your data.

4. Cookies & local storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember your language preference and to keep a working copy of your data for a smoother experience. We use a session token (managed by Supabase Auth) to keep you signed in. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

5. Your rights

You can view, edit, or delete almost everything you've entered directly within the app. You can also permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from your account settings — this cannot be undone. If you have trouble finding an option or want help exporting your data, contact us (below).

6. Children

Koach is not directed at children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach out at support@koach.app.
